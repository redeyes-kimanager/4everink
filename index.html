What we do

Three crafts, one studio. Every design is drawn for you – from a tiny first tattoo to a full back piece.

  • Tattoo

    Custom designs, fine-line and minis, lettering, Japanese and realism, colour work and cover-ups of old ink.

  • Piercing

    Ear, nose and body piercing with a wide choice of jewellery. Helix, daith, conch, septum, navel and more.

  • Cosmetic tattoo

    Permanent make-up for brows – natural shape, drawn to your face.
